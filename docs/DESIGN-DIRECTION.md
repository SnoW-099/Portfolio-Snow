# Snow portfolio: Apple-inspired restraint

The portfolio introduces Angel, a junior developer in Spain, and makes it easy to inspect real projects and contact him.

- System typography: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Helvetica Neue, Segoe UI, sans-serif. No font downloads.
- Light: white background (#ffffff), secondary surface (#f5f5f7), primary text (#1d1d1f), secondary text (#65656b), accent (#0066cc).
- Dark: black background (#000000), secondary surface (#151517), text (#f5f5f7), secondary text (#a1a1a6), accent (#8ab9ff).
- Centered personal introduction; alternating project stories; open, left-aligned information sections.
- Soft corners, thin separators and minimal shadows. Project screenshots retain their original colors.
- One 400ms introduction; no scroll reveals. Reduced motion removes the introduction.
- Appearance selector supports System, Light and Dark, persists the choice and is available in the home, archive and case-study headers.
- Preserve truthful junior positioning, AI disclosures and existing content and project URLs.

Validation includes the existing responsive route checks plus appearance persistence, explicit appearance overriding the system, and shared appearance across routes.
